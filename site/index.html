Trade lost (assumption)

Imports × tariff change ÷ (1 + today's rate) × sensitivity. The sensitivity is your assumption.

0.76

Default from Boehm, Levchenko & Pandalai-Nayar (2023), AER 113(4): short-run elasticity −0.76, long run about −2, defined on the price including the tariff, so the change used is Δtariff ÷ (1 + today's rate). Linear approximation, capped at the value of the trade.
